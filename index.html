Raider Typing Rally

Typing races built from your class’s own words
For teachers

What this site saves: your school email, first name and last initial, your racer, and your race scores, in RJ Ed Tech’s race database (Google Firebase). Leaderboards show only first name and last initial. Only the teacher who made a game sees students’ emails. Anonymous page counts only.
